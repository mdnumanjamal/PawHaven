A little love goes a long way

We believe every paw deserves a place.

PawHaven is a student-project concept for making pet adoption easier to understand and manage. The name is a reminder that adoption changes both lives: the pet gets a home, and the person gets a friend.

Our mission

Help people discover adoptable pets, learn about responsible pet care, and follow an organised adoption-request process.

“Saving one pet may not change the whole world, but for that pet, the world changes forever.”

Our values

  • Kindness: treat every animal with patience and respect.
  • Responsibility: adoption is a long-term commitment.
  • Transparency: share accurate pet information and clear next steps.
  • Safety: protect applicants' information and review requests carefully.

What this platform aims to do

One central place for the adoption journey.

🐾

Pet profiles

Keep pet details, care needs, and availability together.

📋

Application tracking

Help applicants understand the status of their requests.

🤝

Clear communication

Make it easier for adopters and staff to coordinate next steps.
